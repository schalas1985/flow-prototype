# flow prototype

Designs for [flow](https://github.com/schalas1985/flow), put on a public URL so anyone can
open them without an account.

Live: https://schalas1985.github.io/flow-prototype/

## The rule for this repo

Everything here is public. Nothing real goes in it.

No session summaries, no notes, no names of real patients, no tokens, no database ids.
Every number, name and sentence in a prototype here is invented. The pages say so on screen.
The real app, its data and its secrets live in the private `flow` repo and on Vercel, and
nothing from there is copied in here.

## What is in it

`index.html` is the therapist's side of flow, drawn as one canvas instead of a click through.
It works on a tablet: one finger moves the canvas, two fingers pinch to zoom, and a tap opens
a patient, an hour or a resource.
